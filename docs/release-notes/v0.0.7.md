# repsp v0.0.7

Video: sceMpeg and PSMF with blank-frame decoding, and `repsp_codec` for the decoder contracts.

Issue [#13](https://github.com/reexec/repsp/issues/13): the `repsp_codec` target (`AtracDecoder` and `SilentDecoder` moved in, `AvcDecoder` and `BlankAvcDecoder` added); `Scheduler::CallGuestFunction` (calls a guest function on the calling guest thread and preserves its context; waits inside the callback are allowed); guest threads that reach a scheduling point after a stop request now unwind; `repsp_hle_mpeg` (24 sceMpeg functions: PSMF header parsing, MPEG-2 program stream and PES demultiplexing, AVC and ATRAC access unit queues with timestamps, a ring buffer filled through its guest callback); the probe `mpeg_probe`; the knowledge base page `docs/kb/psmf-mpeg.md`. Commits `db67459`…`9c0fe44` on task branch `i013-mpeg`, squashed into this release's merge commit.

Known limits: video decodes to black frames and movie audio to silence (the decoder choice is deferred; statistics show `AVC decode: blank` and `ATRAC decode: silent`).

Verified: Win64 Debug/Release and Linux x64 Debug/Release builds with CTest 18/18. In the consumer reDMP1 the real module ran its movie playback loop and, after skipping it, drew its first primitives and went on to the next library's unimplemented call.
