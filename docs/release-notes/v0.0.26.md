# repsp v0.0.26

A minimum duration for `sceKernelDelayThread`.

Issue [#57](https://github.com/reexec/repsp/issues/57):

- **Why**: on the PSP even a very short delay takes far longer than it asks for (thread switch and kernel timer), and guest polling loops rely on that.
- **Example**: a loop of 1000 × `DelayThread(10 µs)` waiting for another thread lasted 29 ms here and gave up early. In one title it waits for a music thread that checks every 100 ms, so the chart started 60-70 ms before the music.
- **Change**: `sceKernelDelayThread` and `sceKernelDelayThreadCB` now wait at least 200 µs.
- `kernel_sched_probe` checks the registered wait by its deadline.
- The hardware's exact minimum is unconfirmed; 200 µs was chosen by the user's listening.

Measured (one title, same input script):

- Chart start and the first music block are within 0.3 ms of each other (59 ms before).
- Present interval and music underruns are unchanged.

Confirmed by the user.

Verified: build and CTest pass on Win64 (Visual Studio and Ninja) Debug/Release and Linux x64 Debug/Release.
